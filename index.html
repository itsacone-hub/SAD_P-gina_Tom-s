Koobface

Koobface's a trojan that is notable for the method it uses to spread, through social media. If a computer's infected, It'll get info from your account and pretend to be you. Koobface will then send a link to your friends, which is a fake youtube website that informs you to download an adobe flash update. Back in 2009, the USCERT had considered Koobface as a significant risk of a terrorist cyberattack. There are also some variants, like botcert, but didn't make it far after adobe, alongside microsoft and cisco made their move releasing security patches. You can download a sample of the virus to test your Antivirus program on Sophos.

Botnets

A group of infected nodes forming an orchestrated network for offensive purposes. Computers being collectively monitored and managed via command by their host, they're also known as zombie computers. Normally used to exploit mining currency or massive package blows to servers (DDoS).

Brute Force Access

Normally used to crack passwords, a powerful algorithm programs tries different combinations for a password until it matches it. John the Ripper is an example, it uses keywords to get passwords.

DoS & DDoS

Denial Of service is a known form of cyberattack that intends to saturate servers with packages to slow down the service or to fully stop it. Normally as a form of sabotage to the server, often used to create windows for opportunities. DoS is normally done with one computer. DDoS is the large scale version of it, normally using botnet networks.

Trojan

It's a form of malware that presents itself as a legitimate program, like Koobface when it would present itself as an Adobe Flash update. It needs a user to execute it, but once it's done it can be destructive, by using backdoors to bring in more malicious programs, speaking of.

Backdoor

Backdoor is a deliberatelly installed form of access to a computer, bypassing normal security authentification processes. It can be put by a developer for maintaneance reasons or by malware. To spy on a user, to steal infomration or to install malware into the computer.

Spyware

Malware made for surveillance and intel gathering, it tries to go under the radar to steal info and sell it to third parties.

Stealer

It's like a spyware but it's made for more specific info, stealing it from a company to give it their competitors.
